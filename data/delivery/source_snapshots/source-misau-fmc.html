Our Story

Federal Medical Centre (FMC) Misau was established by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu through an approval sent to the Coordinating Minister of Health and Social Welfare, Prof. Muhammad Ali Pate, CON.

The hospital had its pioneer Medical Director appointed in the person of Dr. Rufai Abdu Dachi, a Chief Consultant Haematologist. As a tertiary health facility, FMC Misau has the mandates of providing clinical services, training and research.

A Memorandum of Understanding detailing the ceding of the facility from General Hospital Misau to the Federal Government was signed, marking the official handover. The hospital commenced its full clinical activities, beginning a new chapter in healthcare excellence for the region.

Key Milestones

  • Establishment: Approved by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu
  • Leadership: Appointment of pioneer Medical Director
  • Handover: MOU signing and official handover from Bauchi State Government
  • Operations: Commencement of full clinical activities
