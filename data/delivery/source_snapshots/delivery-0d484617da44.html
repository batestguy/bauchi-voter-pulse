Bauchi Governor To Build Township Roads At Dass

Last updated: September 14, 2024 6:17 pm
By Abubakar Muhammad - Journalist
Share
1 Min Read
SHARE
Follow & Subscribe Us:
Subscribe YouTubeFollow FacebookFollow XFollow Instagram

Governor Bala Abdulkadir Muhammad has initiated the construction of 8.53 km of Dass Township Roads and 12.50 km of Dur-Dado Road.

He disclosed this on Friday at a ceremony in Dass Emir’s palace, Dass local government area of the state.

According to his media aid, Mallam Lawan Mu`azu, the project aims at modernizing Dass Town by reducing congestion and adapting it to contemporary needs.

He said that the project is a major step in the administration’s urban renewal projects across the state.

Governor Muhammad also highlighted the importance of modernization, stating that the projects will “significantly enhance lives and address the challenges of poor road infrastructure.”

He said that his administration’s dedication to urban renewal reflects a strong commitment to improving citizens’ quality of life.
