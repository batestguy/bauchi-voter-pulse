APM Mission

The mission of Allied Peoples' Movement is to promote good governance, empower citizens, and build a better future for all Nigerians.

APM Vision

The vision of APM is to build a united, peaceful, and prosperous Nigeria driven by good leadership, equality, and sustainable development.

APM Constitution

The constitution of Allied Peoples' Movement outlines the party’s principles, structure, rules, and processes for effective leadership and governance.

APM NWC Members

The National Working Committee (NWC) of Allied Peoples' Movement is the leadership body responsible for managing the party’s affairs, implementing policies, coordinating activities, and promoting the party’s vision and objectives across the nation.

Nationhood & Patrotism

Nationhood and patriotism are the catalysts that make a nation great to develop and stand firm. Nations do not become great by chance, miracle..

National Security

The APM shall ensure that Nigeria operates a well structured and efficient national security that will ensure that every Nigerian Citizen's life and property is safe and secured at all times.

Rule of Law

The APM shall strictly operate and govern Nigeria at all levels with strict compliance to the principle of Rule of Law.

Foreign Policy

The APM shall govern Nigeria efficiently and make it a strong nation in the world. Nigeria shall be made a country of first choice in Africa to countries outside African continent in doing business and for tourism.

Federalism

We believes in the system of federalism and shall ensure that Nigeria is run in true federalism and not federalism in name..

Religion
