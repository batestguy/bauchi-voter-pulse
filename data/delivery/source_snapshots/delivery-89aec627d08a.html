The Executive Governor

H.E. Senator Bala Muhammad (Kauran Bauchi)

Senator Bala Abdulkadir Mohammed (born 5 October 1958) is a dedicated Nigerian politician who has served as the Governor of Bauchi State since 2019. Prior to his governorship, he served as the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory from 2010 to 2015, and as a Senator representing Bauchi South from 2007 to 2010.

Key Achievements

  • Urban and rural infrastructure development, including extensive road networks.
  • Radical transformation of the education sector through school renovations and staff monitoring.
  • Significant improvements in healthcare delivery and access to clean water.
  • Implementation of the Violence Against Persons Prohibition (VAPP) law.
  • Construction of the world-class Bauchi Hajj Camp and renovation of traditional palaces.

Our Five Point agenda

✓

Security and Public Safety

We will strengthen community policing, enhance collaboration with security agencies, and deploy technology-driven solutions to improve intelligence gathering and crime prevention. Every citizen deserves to live, work, and raise a family in a safe and peaceful environment.

✓

Quality Education for All

We will invest in modern classrooms, teacher development, digital learning tools, and scholarships to ensure every child has access to quality education. Education remains the foundation for sustainable development and future prosperity.

✓

Infrastructure Development

We will expand road networks, improve water supply systems, promote rural electrification, and modernize public infrastructure across all local government areas. Strong infrastructure is essential for economic growth, investment attraction, and improved quality of life.

✓

Youth and Women Empowerment

We will create opportunities through skills acquisition programs, entrepreneurship support, access to finance, and leadership development initiatives. Our administration will ensure that youth and women are active participants in governance and economic development

✓

Agricultural Transformation and Food Security

We will support farmers with improved inputs, mechanized farming, irrigation systems, access to markets, and agricultural financing. By modernizing agriculture, we will increase food production, create jobs, and strengthen the state's economy.
